# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Stage two
- Split weapon, enemies and effects into independent source modules.
- Add HDR, depth-aware AO filtering, bloom, environment reflection, screen-space sun shafts and heat shimmer.
- Add multiscale rock/stone/metal/cloth materials; improve sand ripples, terrain, rock grounding and harvester details.
- Rebuild first-person weapon; add spring recoil, independent magazine/bolt/support-hand animation, casing ejection and bounded hit decals.
- Add articulated guard locomotion, pre-fire tell, three-shot bursts and damage interruption.
- Add a 36-second gameplay-triggered seismic warning with audio, shake, sand ridge, dust and a HUD graph.
- Add three quality presets, FOV, reduced-motion and moving compass tape.
- Preserve the original six-guard mission, death/retry and extraction loop.
- Record regression, stage-two, default-loop and offline-startup checks for the exact release hash.

## 0.1.0 — Initial playable slice
- Canyon-to-outpost mission, movement, gunplay, two enemy waves and extraction.
- Self-contained build with procedural assets and synthesized sound.
